Show the station a photo of wrapped packs. Every pack gets its own outline, the most specific identity the evidence supports, and its orientation: a line for the long axis, an arrow only when the physical ends can be told apart.

Status: your photos go to the hosted backend. Its pack detector was trained on about 56,000 public retail and grocery photos and found 81% of packs in 2,000 held-out shelf photos. It names five chocolate brands only when it is confident: Cadbury Dairy Milk, Cadbury Gems, KitKat, Bounty and Kinder. On held-out shelf photos about 19 in 20 of the names it gave were right; close-up product photos usually come back as "unknown". Cadbury 5 Star and other brands are not named yet. Reading the results

sku
Exact product confirmed by a barcode or by artwork that only that variant has.
family
The brand is clear, but nothing visible separates the variants.
unknown
A pack, but not one in the catalogue — or not enough evidence.
conflict
A barcode and the artwork disagree. Nothing is promoted until a person checks.
Axis line
The long direction of the pack, 0–180°, measured clockwise from the right.
Arrow
Direction from physical end A to end B, 0–360°. Hidden when the ends can't be told apart.
